Tidy started as a calm, no-clock typing game: a pile of words drops into a room, you type them away one by one, and the pile quietly repeats a few words as it empties. That repetition is exactly what vocabulary needs, so we've already cloned it for [Spanish](/blog/Tidy_Spanish_20261020) and [Japanese](/blog/Tidy_Japanese_20261020).

Now there's a Malay one: [Tidy Bahasa Melayu](/games/tidy-ms).

## What's in it

- **The 3000 most common words.** Taken from a public frequency list built from movie and TV subtitles, so it's everyday spoken Malay (*saya*, *tidak*, *kenapa*, *terima kasih* style words), not textbook Malay. Words under three letters and character names are left out, and the list is cleaned by hand.
- **An English meaning for each word.** When you clear *rumah*, the line `rumah  =  house, home` stays under the room until the next word.
- **No special keys.** Malay uses plain Latin letters, so there are no accents to worry about.
- **It remembers.** Cleared words are saved in your browser, and new rooms deal you words you haven't cleared yet first.

## The honest limitation

Subtitle lists are informal. You'll see casual forms (*nak*, *tak*, *awak*) and some Indonesian-style words alongside standard Malay. That's real spoken language, but it isn't a substitute for a course, reading, or talking to people. Typing gives you exposure and repetition; the rest is up to you.

## Try it

Pick a small room, 30 words is a nice first lap, and let the pile do the repeating. There's more on the method at [Learn Malay by typing](/learn-malay).

Happy typing. *Selamat menaip!*
